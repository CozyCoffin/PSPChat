# PSPChat
A chat room for PSP users! Hope you enjoy!
To install: 
1. Download the EBOOT. (If you don't see it, check the releases page)
2. Make a folder in /PSP/GAME/ named "PSPChat"
3. Put the EBOOT in that folder.
4. If done right, it should be /PSP/GAME/PSPChat/EBOOT.PBP
That's it! The hardest part is over!

Now, you just launch the app from your PSP, make a username, connect to the internet, and you're chatting!

Some basic rules for the chatroom:
1. Certain words are banned. You can, likely, guess which ones. I've been VERY relaxed on the banned words list in hopes that I won't be made to regret it. I know folks have fun, and even bad words can be okay in certain context and situations. Have a good time, but don't set out to push the boundaries.
2. Don't be a dick.

Pretty easy rules to follow. I'm no discord mod. I'm not taking anyone to court over a few comments.
In main chat, if you use one of the banned words, you get banned. The relay is set up to auto-ban anyone who uses words on the banned words list, that includes Cr34t1v3 spelling methods to get around it. The ban's are technically permanent, meaning they won't just go away. If you want to get unbanned, i'll have to unban you manually. I'll, also, see whatever was said that caused the ban which will help me decide if the ban is lifted, or stays. Mistakes happen. The relay may think you're trying to type a banned word when you aren't, and in that case, i'll unban you as soon as possible. In any case, I have faith that I won't really need to ban anyone, so just have fun!
